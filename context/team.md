# Team

Solo. No human team.

Work is handled through AI skills, each acting as one "team member":

| Role | Job | Skill |
|------|-----|-------|
| Job Scout | Scan Indian career pages, score jobs against CV | `job-search` |
| CV Tailor | Tailor CV and cover letter for matched jobs | `job-search` |
| Outreach Drafter | Recruiter and HR messages, follow-ups | `job-search` |
| Tracker | Keep the application tracker updated | `job-search` |
| LinkedIn Writer | Draft posts for inbound leads | `linkedin-post` |
| Client Hunter (later) | Freelance leads | `client-acquisition` |

Revisit when freelancing grows (for example a VA or junior developer).
